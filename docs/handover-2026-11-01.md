## What was done (2026-10-31 night to 11-01)

1. **Stutter on Windows**: the user's NVIDIA Max Frame Rate 117 (for frame generation) let ~117 presents a second
   through on a 120 Hz display; presenting at every refresh missed one in ~40, the game never locked and 13 % of
   pictures were held 1 or 3 refreshes (0.1 too, on the same PC; on 2026-10-27 0.1 was fine: the cap is newer).
   Found with the new `host pacing: measured ... the swapchain reports ...` line and present-timing logs (the
   present call held 16-21 ms inside the driver). Fixes:
   - `c73e07c`: late presents left out of the refresh measurement; a short sample does not end a lock; the driver's
     refresh only within 0.5 % (the first theory, that the driver reported 117.5 Hz, was wrong: it reported 120.000).
   - `93fba4f`: a cap is detected (present calls held over half a refresh, > 0.5 % of 600); then the period is the
     swapchain's and each picture is presented once, timed (60 presents a second under the cap).
   - `bc348df`, `76b1e5c`: the lead before the refresh is 10 ms (`HG_PRESENT_EARLY_MS`; the driver holds an early
     picture to its target time); not locked (144/165 Hz) and capped: a present only per new picture on a steady
     refresh grid; no cap detection without present waits. `HG_PRESENT_REL=0` drops the target time.
   - Measured, 90 s muted runs at x6 widescreen with the cap: 87 % -> 99.51 % (0.6 refresh lead) -> 99.84 % (0.8)
     -> 99.94-99.98 % (10 ms); unlocked mode (HG_LOCK=0) 99.53 %. The user's 7-minute playtest: 99.88 %, "works great".
2. **Movie skip in widescreen** (`4f82de0`): the fade-out after Start darkens the composed frame (8704) with a fill,
   which marked 8704 as an effect pass's buffer, so its copy was not squeezed (stretched wide for a second). Fills
   across 7/8 of the scissor no longer mark. Replay of the user's recording: the fade squeezed; 224 other pictures
   identical.
3. **See-through box with lines** (two F9 marks, VB 5198 and 5617, the courtyard staircase, recording
   `scratch/mark-20261101/rec`): not found. The pictures handed to the window at x6 match native and Soft focus
   Original in structure, no area at native resolution, in-between pictures equal to the real ones. Not in the GS
   picture: maybe the present path (3840x2688 scaled into the window), or the user meant something else. **Asked the
   user where on screen, or a screenshot.**
4. Docs (`6ef0327` and this commit): known issues (frame-rate caps, x6 + Soft focus advice, titled 0.2), the release
   page (new items, the filled table).
